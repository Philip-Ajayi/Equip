## H. MOTION, ANIMATION & BOOK-FLIP INTERACTION SPECIFICATION

### 1. Interactive Page-Turn Mechanics
- **Gesture Trigger:** Horizontal touch swipe, trackpad deltaX, or keyboard ArrowLeft/ArrowRight.
- **Physical States:**
  - *Resting State:* Flat 3D spread, spine curvature at 2.4 degrees, subtle center drop-shadow.
  - *Lift Phase:* Dragging edge causes corner to peel at 35 degrees with dynamic light reflection.
  - *Turn Phase:* Smooth transition using a custom bezier curve: `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`, duration: 420ms.
  - *Settling Phase:* Elastic spring damping (`stiffness: 280, damping: 26`) prevents abrupt stops.
- **Interruption Capability:** Users can reverse their drag gesture at any point prior to the 40% page threshold to cancel the turn without visual jank.
- **Reduced Motion Support:** When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is detected, 3D flips are replaced with a smooth 150ms opacity crossfade.

### 2. Bilingual Stanza Highlighting Interaction
